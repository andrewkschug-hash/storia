# Phase 12J — Luca prima di Roma story design

Design checkpoint only. **No prose. No audio. Not available.**

IDs stay: `luca-prima-di-roma-01` … `05`.

Setting: **Pietralba**, a small fictional town in Lazio. Not Castelbianco (Elena). Not Rome.

Protagonist: **Luca** (shared). Age in these stories: **24**.

Language: A1 **present tense**. No passato prossimo as a teaching goal. Polite **vorrei** (S3) and **mi piace** (S5) are in scope.

Do not retell Luca a Roma (arrival, apartment hunt, Sofia, café job).

## Story 1 — Luca si presenta

**ID:** `luca-prima-di-roma-01`  
**IT / EN:** Luca si presenta / Luca introduces himself  
**Chapters:** 6  
**Shape:** dialogue + identity. Not a day cycle.

**Premise:** At home in Pietralba, Luca says who he is: name, age, family, house, friend Davide. The learner can place Luca as a person before he ever leaves town.

**Why before Rome:** Luca a Roma never teaches introductions, age, or household family. Ch1 assumes a named adult already in motion.

**Prepares Rome:** Name + *essere*/*chiamarsi*/*avere* are in place so Ch1 can start at the station without a self-intro lesson.

### Chapters

| # | Title IT (working) | Primary | Secondary | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ciao, sono Luca | introductions | social | Name, *mi chiamo*, io/tu, ciao/buongiorno |
| 2 | Quanti anni hai? | age | numbers | Luca is 24; Chiara is 16; numbers 1–30 |
| 3 | La mia famiglia | family | introductions | Mamma Marta, papà Paolo, sorella, nonna Lidia; *mio/mia* |
| 4 | Casa mia | descriptions | family, numbers | Rooms: cucina, camera, soggiorno; *c’è / ci sono*; how many rooms |
| 5 | Questo è Davide | social | introductions, descriptions | Friend: lui/lei, *è alto/simpatico*, di Pietralba |
| 6 | I numeri in casa | numbers | family | Phone digits, civic number, how many people at dinner |

**Vocab themes:** names, greetings, family nouns, numbers 0–30, rooms, simple adjectives (grande/piccolo, alto/basso).

**Grammar:** *essere*, *chiamarsi*, *avere*, articles, *mio/mia/tuo*, *c’è/ci sono*, subject pronouns.

**Comprehension:** who is who; ages; where people live; count people/rooms.

**Do not:** leave town; mention Rome as destination yet; introduce Sofia.

## Story 2 — Una giornata di Luca

**ID:** `luca-prima-di-roma-02`  
**IT / EN:** Una giornata di Luca / A day in Luca’s life  
**Chapters:** 7  
**Shape:** one day, **clock-driven**. The only full giornata story.

**Premise:** Morning to evening in Pietralba with times, days, dates, and Chiara’s school timetable. Luca helps at home; he does not have the Rome café job.

**Why before Rome:** Luca a Roma uses *mattina/oggi/domani* but not clock hours, weekdays as a set, or calendar dates.

**Prepares Rome:** Time and “when” language so later Rome days (“oggi Luca cammina”) sit on a real A1 clock/calendar.

### Chapters

| # | Title IT (working) | Primary | Secondary | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Che ore sono? | clock | numbers | Analog/simple digital: *è l’una*, *sono le otto*, *e mezzo* |
| 2 | La mattina | daily_routine | clock, food | Wake, breakfast (light food only), times |
| 3 | I giorni della settimana | days | schedules | Today is lunedì; ieri/domani as labels, still present tense |
| 4 | Il calendario | dates | days, numbers | Day + month; *che giorno è oggi?* |
| 5 | L’orario di Chiara | schedules | clock, days | School timetable; Luca’s simple morning plan |
| 6 | Il pomeriggio | daily_routine | clock, family | Luca helps Paolo / accompanies Chiara — timed beats, not a job plot |
| 7 | La sera | daily_routine | clock, family | Dinner time; *a che ora*; tomorrow’s plan |

**Vocab themes:** hours, *alle*, days, months, *prima/dopo/poi*, routine verbs (*svegliarsi, alzarsi, fare colazione, lavorare, cenare*).

**Grammar:** *a/alle* + time; reflexives (A1 routine); *ogni*; sequence adverbs. No past-tense teaching.

**Comprehension:** what time; which day; sequence of the day; Chiara’s schedule vs Luca’s.

**Do not:** clone this structure in stories 3–5; start a Rome career.

## Story 3 — Al supermercato

**ID:** `luca-prima-di-roma-03`  
**IT / EN:** Al supermercato / At the supermarket  
**Chapters:** 6  
**Shape:** task — list → counter → pay → missing item.

**Premise:** Marta sends Luca with a list. He buys food, asks prices, uses quantities, talks to Signora Pina. One shop, one job to finish.

**Why before Rome:** Rome has *soldi/costare* and café hunger, not shopping, euros, or quantities.

**Prepares Rome:** *vorrei*, *quanto costa*, food nouns — useful at Bar Centrale without making Ch1 a shopping lesson.

### Chapters

| # | Title IT (working) | Primary | Secondary | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | La lista | food | quantities | List: pane, latte, pasta, frutta; *un po’*, due |
| 2 | Al banco | ordering | food, social | *Vorrei…*, *per favore*, *altro?* |
| 3 | Quanto costa? | prices | numbers | Euro; *costa / costano*; too much / OK |
| 4 | Quanto ne prendo? | quantities | food, prices | *etto, chilo, bottiglia, pacco* |
| 5 | Si può pagare? | shopping | prices, social | Checkout, cash/card, *lo scontrino* |
| 6 | Manca qualcosa | shopping | likes, food | Forgot an item; *mi piace / non mi piace* seed |

**Vocab themes:** food, euro, *caro/economico*, quantities, checkout phrases.

**Grammar:** *vorrei*; *questo/quello*; *quanto/quanti*; partitive *un po’ di*; polite *scusi*.

**Comprehension:** what is on the list; price comparisons; how much to buy; what is missing.

**Do not:** turn this into a town tour (that is story 4).

## Story 4 — In paese

**ID:** `luca-prima-di-roma-04`  
**IT / EN:** In paese / Around town  
**Chapters:** 7  
**Shape:** spatial — places, getting around, weather. Not “wake up / come home.”

**Premise:** Luca must reach the pharmacy and then meet Davide. He asks directions, checks the weather, takes the bus, almost gets lost, and sees the **local** station (plant for story 5 — he does not board for Rome).

**Why before Rome:** Rome streets are atmosphere, not A1 *a destra / prendi l’autobus / che tempo fa*.

**Prepares Rome:** Asking where something is; transport words. Story 5 will use the same local station for goodbye. Luca a Roma Ch1 is arrival in **Rome**, different station, different city.

### Chapters

| # | Title IT (working) | Primary | Secondary | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dov’è la piazza? | places | descriptions | Piazza, via, farmacia, parco, bar |
| 2 | A destra, a sinistra | directions | places | Route to the farmacia |
| 3 | Che tempo fa? | weather | descriptions | *Fa caldo/freddo*, piove, c’è il sole |
| 4 | Le stagioni | seasons | weather | Spring/summer/autumn/winter; today is… |
| 5 | L’autobus | transportation | schedules, clock | Stop, ticket, *orario*, *prossima fermata* |
| 6 | Siamo persi | directions | social, places | Ask Franco / a passer-by; *dov’è…?* |
| 7 | In stazione | transportation | places, schedules | Pietralba station, *binario*, train times — **no departure to Rome** |

**Vocab themes:** town places, left/right/straight, weather adjectives, seasons, bus/train A1 set.

**Grammar:** *dov’è / dove sono*; prepositions *a/in/da/per*; *prendere* + transport; impersonal *fa* + weather.

**Comprehension:** map-like where; follow a direction; weather choice; which bus/time.

**Do not:** arrive in Rome; use location id `stazione` (Rome). Use `stazione-pietralba`.

## Story 5 — La festa di Luca

**ID:** `luca-prima-di-roma-05`  
**IT / EN:** La festa di Luca / Luca’s party  
**Chapters:** 6  
**Shape:** social event → decision → goodbye. Bridge to Luca a Roma, not a rewrite of Ch1.

**Premise:** Luca’s birthday. Invitations, likes, family prep, party with Davide and Elisa. After the party he tells them: tomorrow he leaves for Rome. Last beat: Pietralba station goodbye; he is on the train. **Stop before Rome.**

**Why before Rome:** Birthdays, invitations, and *mi piace* are missing from Luca A1. The trip motive can exist without duplicating arrival hunger / Sofia / Bar Centrale.

**Prepares Rome:** Learner knows Luca chose to go; family/friends exist offstage. Ch1 can open *Luca arriva a Roma* as the next moment, not a second goodbye scene.

### Chapters

| # | Title IT (working) | Primary | Secondary | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Il mio compleanno | birthdays | dates, age | Date of birth; *quanti anni*; *auguri* preview |
| 2 | Ti invito | invitations | days, social | *Vieni sabato?*; Davide yes, Elisa maybe |
| 3 | Cosa ti piace? | likes | food | Music/food likes for the party |
| 4 | Prepariamo la festa | birthdays | family, quantities, food | Prep with Marta; how much cake/drinks |
| 5 | Alla festa | social | birthdays, introductions | Guests, *piacere*, *auguri*, small talk |
| 6 | Domani parto | social | transportation, family | Decision + goodbye at **stazione-pietralba**; on the train; **do not show Rome** |

**Vocab themes:** compleanno, invito, *mi piace/piacciono*, festa, *partire*, *arrivederci*.

**Grammar:** *mi piace* + noun/infinitive; *vuoi / vieni*; *domani* + present for near future.

**Comprehension:** who is invited; what Luca likes; what happens at the party; that he leaves tomorrow (not that he has arrived).

**Hard stop vs Luca a Roma Ch1:** no Termini, no *Luca arriva a Roma*, no bar, no Sofia, no *fame* arrival beat. Those remain Ch1 exclusive.

## Vocabulary progression (themes, not lemma lists)

Authoring must add new A1 lemmas per story, not rephrase Rome vocab.

1. **S1** — identity, family, house, 0–30  
2. **S2** — clock, week, months, routine verbs  
3. **S3** — food set, euro, quantities, shop verbs  
4. **S4** — town map, directions, weather/seasons, bus/train  
5. **S5** — likes, invite, party, *partire*

Reuse across pre-Rome is reinforcement (family names, *euro*, *dov’è*), not the only content.

Target length when authored: A1 ~150–350 words/chapter (existing CEFR doc). Questions: 2–4, story-local ids.

Suggested chapter ids: `luca-prima-di-roma-01-01` … not `luca-a-roma-01`.

```
S1 who Luca is
 → S2 ordinary time/life
 → S3 practical transactions
 → S4 world beyond the house (local station planted)
```
