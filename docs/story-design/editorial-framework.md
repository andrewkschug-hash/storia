# Storibase Editorial & Story-Design Framework (A2+)

**Status:** FROZEN / VALIDATED (Phase 13)  
**Applies to:** *Il villaggio che non esiste*, *Una lettera per Elena*, *La casa delle finestre*, and all future A2+ narrative pathways.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Narrative Information Engines

> **The Golden Rule:**  
> A chapter does not earn its place merely because something happens. The learner's understanding of the story must be different when they leave it than when they entered.

Language learners at the A2+ level do not need static graded readers where plot is merely a decorative coat of paint over vocabulary lists. They need **dynamic information engines** that generate forward momentum through cognitive engagement, deduction, and dramatic tension.

### The Product Triangle

```
                       THE PHASE 13 COGNITIVE TRIANGLE

                           [ Casa delle finestre ]
                           Engine: Pattern & Anomaly
                           Question: "What is happening?"
                           Learner Task: Observe & Decode
                           Dominant Arc: Pattern → Ethical Choice
                                     ▲
                                    / \
                                   /   \
                                  /     \
                                 /       \
  [ Una lettera per Elena ]     ◄─────────►     [ Il villaggio che non esiste ]
  Engine: Interpersonal Irony                   Engine: Evidence Reconstruction
  Question: "What do they mean?"                Question: "What happened here?"
  Learner Task: Interpret & Subtext             Learner Task: Re-evaluate Reality
  Dominant Arc: Subtext → Vocal Courage         Dominant Arc: Evidence → Reinterpretation
```

### The Universal Protagonist Arc
Underneath the genre-specific engines, all three pathways share an empowering learner-centric progression:
```
  [ Observe ] ──► [ Interpret ] ──► [ Re-evaluate ] ──► [ Act ] ──► [ Integrate ]
```
* **Giada (*Collevento*):** Finds evidence → Reconstructs village history → Recognizes institutional boundaries → Chooses what she can/cannot save → Integrates memory (*"Esisteva"*).
* **Elena (*Elena*):** Writes anonymously → Discovers Pietro's identity → Recognizes paper as a crutch → Speaks aloud in reality → Integrates the book into daily life.
* **Irene (*Casa*):** Maps window grid → Uncovers suppressed records → Crosses ethical/legal boundary → Secures digital duplicate → Exits as a principled witness.

## 2. The 3 Layers of Belief Model

Every chapter operates across three interconnected layers of belief. A strong chapter shifts at least one layer; a major narrative pivot shifts two or three:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. WORLD BELIEF                                                        │
│ What does the character/learner believe is true about reality, the     │
│ setting, physical rules, institutions, and the central mystery?        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. RELATIONSHIP BELIEF                                                 │
│ What does the character/learner believe about another person's         │
│ motives, trustworthiness, emotional state, and dynamic?                │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. SELF BELIEF                                                         │
│ What does the character/learner believe about their own agency,        │
│ vulnerability, identity, security, and what they are capable of?       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. The Belief-Shift & Explanation Tests

### Test 1: The Belief-Shift Test
> *"What did the learner/character believe at Line 1 that they no longer believe at the final line of the chapter?"*

### Test 2: The Explanation Test
> *"Is this chapter changing what the learner believes, or merely explaining something they already learned?"*
* **Supported Explanation:** The explanation materially alters the emotional or narrative stakes of what immediately follows (e.g. *Elena* Ch 10 setting up Ch 11; *Collevento* Ch 11 setting up the dam perimeter).
* **Static Exposition:** Catching the reader up without altering future stakes (**Flagged for cut/merge**).

### 5-Way Chapter Classification Taxonomy

| Classification | Definition | Narrative Function | Health Check |
|:--------------:|:-----------|:-------------------|:-------------|
| **`SHIFT`** | Changes the learner's mental model (core belief delta). | Major Pivot / Escalation | Essential across every 2–3 chapter block. |
| **`DEEPEN`** | Adds emotional, psychological, or contextual weight to an existing model. | Escalation / Atmosphere | Vital for character resonance; avoid back-to-back without action. |
| **`CONSEQUENCE`** | Forces an action, choice, or practical fallout resulting from previous shifts. | Action / Fallout | Keeps plot reactive, urgent, and alive. |
| **`EXPLANATION`** | Primarily explains or rationalizes something already established. | Context / Lore | Must be *Supported Explanation*. **2+ consecutive creates drag.** |
| **`STALL`** | Neither changes nor meaningfully deepens anything. | Filler | **Forbidden (0% tolerance).** |

### Scoring Metric (0 / 1 / 2)

| Score | Meaning | Editorial Assessment |
|:-----:|:--------|:---------------------|
| **0** | **Stalled Chapter** | No meaningful change in understanding. Only surface action or filler word count. **Must be cut, merged, or rewritten.** |
| **1** | **Informational / Contextual Shift** | A new fact, clue, or psychological nuance deepens understanding and sharpens the question. |
| **2** | **Transformational Shift** | A material pivot in worldview, relationship definition, or narrative reality. Prior clues change meaning. |

## 4. Key Editorial Heuristics & Restraint Rules

### 1. The Organic Meaning-Shift Rule (Anti-Formulaic)
The meaning of the central mystery, relationship, or problem must **materially change across every 2–3 chapter unit**.
* *Crucial Clarification:* This is **not** a mechanical mandate for a Score-2 twist on a rigid timer. A meaningful shift often emerges from a `DEEPEN` + `CONSEQUENCE` sequence just as effectively as from a single sharp `SHIFT`.
* *Collevento Example:* Moves from *supernatural impossibility* (road turns to water) to *physical mechanism* (dam sluice schedule) to *social/administrative eraser* (erased from maps by human decision).
* *Elena Example:* Moves from *written sanctuary* (safe notebook) to *paralyzing crutch* (fear of speech) to *courageous spoken reality* (storeroom and Thursday visit).
* *Casa Example:* Moves from *nocturnal light puzzle* to *closed school schedule* to *suppressed structural cracks* to *Friday shredder countdown*.

### 2. Restraint Over Lore Dumps (The Anti-Ch 14 Rule)
No single character is an omniscient exposition machine.
* *Wrong:* Neri sits down in Ch 14 and explains the dam, the 1978 history, the maps, and the village secrets in one speech.
* *Right:* Neri shares his local, partially flawed folklore. Giada's mother's documents contradict part of it. Now both have a better, sharper question.

### 3. Dramatic Irony Without Melodrama (The Anti-Ch 11 Rule)
Emotional and identity reveals must happen in ordinary, physical settings.
* *Wrong:* Pietro dramatically repeats *"oggi ho paura del silenzio"* while staring intensely into Elena's eyes.
* *Right:* Pietro remarks casually about the hum of the refrigerator. Elena recognizes the sentence from the book. Pietro has no idea what just happened.

### 4. Grounded Adult Resolutions (The Anti-Conquest Rule)
Endings are **exits and understandings**, not superhero victories.
* *Collevento (Ch 23/24):* Resolves with bread, dishes, father, quiet kitchen routine, and the simple, undeniable realization: *“Esisteva.”*
* *Elena (Ch 22):* Resolves with Thursday morning, ordinary work, fixing the wobbly table leg with a wooden wedge, and *“Sergio oggi fa fisioterapia. Io passo dopo.”*
* *Casa (Ch 23/24):* Resolves with closing the notebook, contract quietly expired, boarding the bus as a principled witness.

## 6. Phase 14 Editorial Production Audit Checklist

When auditing the actual Italian prose of any chapter against its matrix entry, apply these 7 criteria:

1. **Belief-Shift Delivery:** Does the prose actually dramatize and establish the stated opening-to-closing belief shift?
2. **Dramatized Catalyst:** Is the catalyst concretely enacted in scene action rather than narrated in abstract exposition?
3. **Classification Accuracy:** Is the assigned class (`SHIFT`, `DEEPEN`, `CONSEQUENCE`, `EXPLANATION`) confirmed by the scene's actual execution?
4. **CEFR & Language Ceiling:** Does the vocabulary, sentence length, and grammatical complexity match the A2+ tier targets?
5. **Exposition Discipline:** Is the chapter stripped of unearned lore dumps, poetic filler, or repetitive recaps?
6. **Final Beat Resonance:** Does the closing sentence or micro-action crystallize the new understanding?
7. **Continuity & Capitalization:** Does the *subsequent* chapter immediately recognize and build upon the change, rather than resetting to baseline?
